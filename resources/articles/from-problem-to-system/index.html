From business problem to working system.

A technology project is stronger when the business problem, intended outcome and implementation path are clear from the beginning.

1. Discover

Identify the current process, the friction it creates and the people it affects. A clear problem statement makes it easier to distinguish a useful system from an attractive but unnecessary one.

2. Design

Consider the process, information, integrations and adoption required. The solution may involve existing applications, process changes, a tailored build or a combination of each.

3. Deliver

Implementation should include the people who will work with the system. The goal is a reliable improvement to day-to-day work, not technology that sits apart from it.
