Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about Widberz and the technology work we do.

What does Widberz do?

Widberz is a business technology, systems and software company. We advise businesses on technology and systems, build digital products, and help implement practical solutions.

What does Widberz build?

We build business and corporate websites, web applications, mobile applications, portals, custom software, integrations and database-backed applications.

What is WCPS?

WCPS is Widberz’s Clinic Practice System for modern healthcare providers. It manages patients, billing and clinic operations in one system.

Is Widberz a Zoho Consulting Partner?

Yes. Widberz helps businesses evaluate, implement and optimise Zoho business applications as part of a broader digital transformation journey.

Does Widberz sell traditional hosting packages?

No. Widberz provides Infrastructure & Deployment support, helping clients deploy and manage websites and applications on reliable cloud or VPS infrastructure.
