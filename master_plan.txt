# Widberz.com — Website Restructuring & Repositioning Plan

**Project:** Widberz Consulting Website
**Repository:** `widber20/widberz.com`
**Approach:** Refocus and restructure — not a visual redesign
**Primary objective:** Align the website with the current strategic direction of Widberz Consulting.

---

## 1. Strategic Direction

Widberz should move away from being presented as a broad SME support business and position itself primarily as a **business technology, systems and software company**.

### Core positioning

> **Widberz helps businesses design, build and implement practical digital systems that make work simpler, smarter and more scalable.**

The website should communicate four capabilities:

1. **We build technology.**
2. **We implement technology.**
3. **We advise businesses on technology and systems.**
4. **We have our own healthcare technology product — WCPS.**

---

# 2. Services to Retain

## A. Web & App Development

This becomes one of Widberz's primary commercial offerings.

### Include

* Business websites
* Corporate websites
* Web applications
* Mobile applications
* Business portals
* Patient/customer portals
* Custom software
* API development
* System integrations
* Database-backed applications

### Positioning

> We design and build websites, web applications and mobile solutions that solve real business problems.

Avoid presenting this simply as "website design."

The emphasis should be on **digital solutions**, not just visual websites.

---

# 3. IT & Business Consulting

Retain this as a major offering.

### Include

* IT consulting
* Business process analysis
* Digital transformation
* Systems implementation
* Process optimisation
* Automation
* Technology assessment
* Project management
* Change management
* Business systems strategy

### Positioning

> We help businesses understand where technology can improve the way they operate — and then help them implement it.

This is one of Widberz's strongest differentiators because it connects the user's business problem to the technology solution.

---

# 4. WCPS — Healthcare Technology

WCPS should receive **first-class treatment** rather than being buried inside services.

WCPS is not simply another consulting project.

It is:

> **Widberz's own healthcare technology platform.**

### WCPS positioning

> **WCPS — Clinic Management Software**
>
> A practical digital platform helping healthcare practices manage patients, appointments, clinical records, billing, claims and communications.

### Website treatment

WCPS should have its own top-level navigation item.

Suggested structure:

* WCPS

  * Overview
  * Features
  * For Clinics
  * Request a Demo

The homepage should also feature WCPS prominently.

The goal is for a visitor to understand:

> Widberz doesn't only advise businesses about technology — **we build and operate technology ourselves.**

---

# 5. Zoho Partner

Zoho must remain visible because Widberz continues to be a **Zoho Consulting Partner** and has contractual obligations.

However, Zoho should not dominate the Widberz identity.

### Positioning

> **Zoho Consulting Partner**
>
> Widberz helps businesses evaluate, implement and optimise Zoho's suite of business applications.

### Important distinction

Widberz is:

> **A business technology company that is also a Zoho Consulting Partner.**

Not:

> A Zoho company that happens to provide other services.

### Zoho page

Create/retain a dedicated Zoho page containing:

* Widberz's Zoho partnership
* Brief explanation of implementation/consulting capability
* Relevant Zoho products
* Links to official Zoho product pages

### Product links

Where individual Zoho products are mentioned, link visitors to the **official Zoho website/product pages**.

Do not attempt to recreate detailed Zoho product documentation on the Widberz site.

This keeps the information current and fulfils the partner relationship cleanly.

---

# 6. Infrastructure & Deployment

Remove **Web Hosting** as a product/service category.

Replace it with:

> **Infrastructure & Deployment**

The philosophy should be transparent and customer-centric.

### Positioning

> We help businesses deploy their websites and applications on reliable cloud and VPS infrastructure — whether you choose to manage your own infrastructure or have Widberz manage it for you.

### Possible services

* VPS deployment
* Cloud deployment
* Website deployment
* Application deployment
* Server configuration
* Domain/DNS configuration
* SSL
* Backups
* Monitoring
* Security configuration
* Server management

### Important commercial philosophy

Do not sell:

> "Widberz Hosting — Rxxx/month"

Instead:

> **Your infrastructure. Your choice. Our expertise.**

Clients may:

1. Purchase their own VPS and manage it themselves.
2. Purchase their own VPS and have Widberz deploy it.
3. Have Widberz manage the infrastructure.
4. Engage Widberz for ongoing technical support.

This creates transparency and avoids Widberz carrying unnecessary infrastructure costs.

---

# 7. Offerings to Remove

Remove these from the primary Widberz offering structure:

### ❌ Accounting / Bookkeeping

Remove:

* Bookkeeping Services
* Accounting packages
* ProfitBooks positioning
* Accounting-related pricing
* Accounting CTAs

### ❌ Administrative Services

Remove administrative support as a service offering.

### ❌ Traditional Hosting Packages

Remove:

* Hosting Starter
* Hosting Standard
* Hosting Ultimate
* Hosting pricing tables
* Hosting reseller positioning

Replace with Infrastructure & Deployment where appropriate.

# 12. Existing Content Audit

Before editing code, create a simple inventory of the existing site.

For every page/section record:

| Existing Item            | Action              |
| ------------------------ | ------------------- |
| IT & Business Consulting | KEEP / REWRITE      |
| Brand & Digital Services | RESTRUCTURE         |
| Bookkeeping              | REMOVE              |
| Zoho Services            | RETAIN / REPOSITION |
| Web Hosting              | REMOVE / REPLACE    |
| Existing About           | REVIEW              |
| Existing Contact         | KEEP                |
| Testimonials             | KEEP / UPDATE       |
| Existing portfolio       | REVIEW              |
| Existing homepage        | RESTRUCTURE         |

Do not delete content immediately.

First determine whether useful material can be repurposed.

---

# 13. Link & URL Strategy

Because the current website is already live, do not casually delete URLs.

For every page being removed:

### Option A — Redirect

If the content has a logical replacement.

Example:

```text
/bookkeeping
        ↓
/services
```

### Option B — Consolidate

Move useful content into a broader service page.

Example:

```text
/zoho-services
        ↓
/zoho
```

### Option C — Retire

Only when the page has no continuing strategic or SEO value.

---

# 14. SEO Preservation

Before changing pages:

Record:

* Current page titles
* Meta descriptions
* URLs
* H1 headings
* Important keywords
* Existing indexed pages
* Sitemap
* Robots.txt

Avoid unnecessary URL changes.

Where URLs must change, use redirects.

The goal is:

> **Improve positioning without unnecessarily destroying existing search equity.**

# 16. Git Workflow

Before making changes:

### Step 1

Create a clean baseline.

```bash
git status
```

### Step 2

Commit the current production state if necessary.

```bash
git add .
git commit -m "chore: establish website restructuring baseline"
```

### Step 3

Create a working branch.

```bash
git checkout -b feature/widberz-repositioning
```

Do not make the restructuring directly on the production branch.

# 18. Success Criteria

The restructuring is successful if a new visitor can answer these questions within approximately 10 seconds:

### What does Widberz do?

> Business technology, software and consulting.

### What does Widberz build?

> Websites, applications and business systems.

### What does Widberz consult on?

> Technology, systems, processes and digital transformation.

### What is WCPS?

> Widberz's healthcare technology platform.

### What is the Zoho relationship?

> Widberz is a Zoho Consulting Partner.

### Does Widberz provide hosting?

Not as a traditional hosting reseller.

Instead:

> Widberz helps clients deploy and manage their own infrastructure.

```text
                    WIDBERZ
                       │
             Business Technology
                       │
        ┌──────────────┼──────────────┐
        │              │              │
     CONSULT          BUILD        IMPLEMENT
        │              │              │
 IT & Business    Web & Apps     Systems /
   Consulting                    Infrastructure
        │              │              │
        └──────────────┼──────────────┘
                       │
                     WCPS
             Our own technology
                       │
                    ZOHO
              Partner ecosystem
```

This is a much stronger story than:

```text
IT + Websites + Hosting + Bookkeeping + Admin
```

---

# 20. Guiding Principle

**Do not redesign Widberz. Reposition Widberz.**

Keep the existing visual identity and much of the existing design.

Remove distractions.

Strengthen the technology story.

Make WCPS visible.

Keep the Zoho partnership visible without making it the identity of the company.

Replace commodity services with higher-value technical services.

And make the website reflect the business Widberz is becoming — rather than the collection of services it has accumulated over time.
